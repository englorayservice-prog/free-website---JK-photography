About me

The eye behind JK Photography

I'm KA Karthik — a photographer from Usilampatti, Madurai who believes a wedding is not a photoshoot, it's a once-in-a-lifetime story.

Over the years, team JK has covered candid weddings, grand traditional days, couple shoots and every home function in between — from the muhurtham to the send-off, from the ground to the sky with drone shots. Our reels travel across Tamil Nadu; our favourite compliment is a family that calls us back for the next function.
